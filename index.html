ATRIUM Social VR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

ATRIUM Social VR ("ATRIUM", app id com.encore.resortvr) is a multiplayer social virtual-reality resort for Meta Quest. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Massimiliano Amatulli, Italy.
Contact: amatulli.massimiliano@gmail.com

2. What data the app uses and why

DataWhy it is used
Meta user IDTo load your Meta Avatar and show it to you and to the other players in the same session.
Meta Avatar (appearance)To display your avatar inside the resort.
Head and hand position (headset and controller tracking)Sent in real time to the other players in your session so they can see your avatar move.
Voice (microphone)Real-time voice chat with nearby players. Voice is streamed live and is not recorded or stored by ATRIUM. You can mute your microphone at any time from the in-app menu.
Display name you choose in the appShown above your avatar to other players.
Anonymous player ID created by Unity AuthenticationNeeded to connect you to multiplayer sessions and voice chat.
App settings (volume, seated/standing, muted players)Saved only on your headset.

ATRIUM does not use your data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not keep it on its own servers. ATRIUM has no servers of its own: all multiplayer and voice traffic goes through the service providers below.

3. Service providers

Unity Technologies provides the multiplayer network (Unity Relay / Distributed Authority, Lobby/Sessions, Authentication) and voice chat (Vivox). Unity processes this data only to provide these services. Depending on where players are, data may be processed in the United States, the Netherlands, Finland, Poland, Germany, Brazil, Singapore, Japan, India or Australia. See Unity's Privacy Policy and Unity's sub-processors.

Meta Platforms provides your user ID and avatar through the Meta Horizon platform. See Meta's Privacy Policy.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the providers' own safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

4. How long data is kept

ATRIUM does not store your data. Position, voice and avatar data exist only while you are in a session. Settings stay on your headset until you uninstall the app. Data kept by Unity or Meta follows their own policies.

5. Legal basis

The data is used because it is necessary to provide the multiplayer service you ask for when you use the app (performance of a contract, art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to data portability. Write to the contact above. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

7. Children

ATRIUM is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Players must meet the minimum age required by Meta for Meta Quest accounts.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
